One directory, several decisions

The Fashionreps Litbuy spreadsheet is a web-based product discovery resource. It gives you a place to start browsing. This companion focuses on the decisions that come after finding a listing.

Keep discovery, ordering and shipping separate in your notes. A reference price in a catalog is not a delivered total, and a product image is not proof of quality or authenticity. Orders and payments are handled on your selected agent's website.

Build a shortlist you can revisit

Save the exact seller URL, listing identifier if available, variant and date checked. Add the question that must be answered next. A short list with clear gaps is more useful than a long list of unexplained links.

What to record for each candidate
FieldWhat it helps you check
Source & variantWhether the link still opens the intended item, color, size and quantity.
Price & currencyWhat the current listing actually charges, distinct from shipping and fees.
Fit informationMeasurements and units you can compare with an item that fits.
Evidence & gapsRelevant photo source, stated date and unanswered questions.
Next actionAsk for a measurement, confirm availability or remove the candidate.

For clothing, distinguish flat widths from circumferences. For shoes, clarify the measuring method rather than relying only on a size label. Similar photos across two listings do not establish that they offer the same item.

Choose Litbuy explicitly

  1. Open the Litbuy directory on Fashionreps.
  2. Use Use Litbuy for product links on that page or select Litbuy in the shopping-agent selector.
  3. Confirm the current agent before opening a product purchase link, including after browsing another category.
  4. Check the destination product, variant, quantity and currency on the agent's site.
  5. Review current costs and terms there before paying.
The topic of a page and your saved agent preference are different things. Do not assume that reading a Litbuy guide automatically changes the selected agent.

ShoesWhich sizing method is documented?HoodiesHow do chest width and length compare?T-shirtsIs this the intended color, print and cut?JacketsAre lining, closures and measurements clear?PantsAre waist, rise and inseam measured consistently?AccessoriesWhat dimensions and parts are included?JerseysWhat sizing and customization details apply?

Category links open Fashionreps. Check your selected agent there before following purchase links.

Review each warehouse item before grouping it

  • Match: Connect each received item to its source URL and ordered variant.
  • Inspect: Check count, visible condition, seams, closures and any requested measurements.
  • Resolve: Write unanswered questions down. Request another view or measurement when useful.
  • Check time limits: Review current storage and return deadlines in your own account.
  • Decide: Compare your available options before approving onward shipping.
QC photos provide limited visual evidence. Lighting changes apparent color; photos alone cannot establish material composition or long-term durability. Evidence coverage and current account policies must be checked individually.

Plan the parcel, not just the product price

Record the costs that apply to your order in one currency. Leave missing amounts marked as unknown instead of silently counting them as zero.

Items and quantities
+ seller-to-warehouse delivery
+ applicable service fees
+ current international shipping estimate
+ payment/currency charges
+ applicable taxes or duties
= estimated delivered cost

Ask how parcel dimensions, billable weight, packaging and your actual route affect the estimate. Confirm whether the items can travel together and whether the destination is eligible for the selected line.

Consolidation is an option to evaluate, not a guarantee of cheaper shipping. Changing packaging may change protection as well as parcel size. Obtain a current quote and understand the tradeoff before choosing.

A decision note with visible unknowns

This example illustrates planning, not a completed purchase or a product recommendation.

“Two clothing items shortlisted. One size chart clarified; one sleeve measurement pending. Litbuy selected. Warehouse photos not yet available. Parcel estimate unknown.”

The next action is clear: resolve the missing measurement. Later, update the same note with arrival status, the evidence reviewed, applicable deadlines and a current parcel estimate. Keep private order details in your own records.

Common questions

Is this an Excel or Google Sheets file?

No. This page is a practical guide to the Fashionreps web catalog and the decisions around an agent order.

Is this official Litbuy documentation?

No. It is an independent guide. Confirm current service terms and account policies with Litbuy. Brand names identify the services and do not imply endorsement.

Can I assume a quoted storage period still applies?

Check your current account terms and the deadlines for your actual items. This guide does not promise a particular storage entitlement or fee.

Does combining items always reduce shipping?

No saving is guaranteed. Compare the current quote, route eligibility, packaging and billable-weight method for the proposed parcel.

How do I handle an outdated listing?

Reopen the source and verify the intended variant. Report the URL, actual result and date checked through the repository's Issues tab. Do not post private account or payment information.
