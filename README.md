# Litbuy Spreadsheet — A Find-to-Parcel Guide

Use the [Fashionreps Litbuy spreadsheet](https://fashion-reps.net/litbuy-spreadsheet) to discover listings, then keep a clear shortlist before making an order decision. This repository explains how to move from saved finds to warehouse review and parcel planning.

**[Browse the Litbuy catalog →](https://fashion-reps.net/litbuy-spreadsheet)** · [Read the practical guide](https://fashionrep.github.io/Litbuy-Spreadsheet/)

## Make a useful shortlist

Save the exact seller URL and selected variant. For each candidate, record the date checked, reference price and currency, missing information and your next action. A listing photo is a starting point; it does not establish quality, authenticity or suitability.

| Item | Keep in your notes | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A product find | Source URL, size, color, quantity | Reopen the source and confirm the variant |
| Fit information | Units and measurements from the seller | Compare with a similar item you own |
| Evidence | Image source, relevant variant, stated date | Ask for missing views or measurements |
| Budget | Item cost and delivery/fee assumptions | Obtain current amounts from the agent |
| Warehouse item | Arrival date, status, unresolved questions | Check deadlines and available options |

## Select Litbuy explicitly

Open the Litbuy page on Fashionreps and use its **Use Litbuy for product links** control or the shopping-agent selector. Check the selected agent before opening a purchase link, including after browsing another category. The page you are reading and your saved agent preference are different things.

Before paying, confirm the destination listing, variant, quantity, currency and current terms on the agent's site. Purchases and shipping are handled there, not in this repository.

## Warehouse review before consolidation

1. Match each received item to its source URL and ordered variant.
2. Check the count, visible condition, seams, closures and any requested measurements.
3. Write unresolved questions explicitly. Request another angle or measurement when useful.
4. Check current storage and return deadlines in your account; do not assume a directory's policy summary remains current.
5. Review destination eligibility, packaging options and a current shipping estimate before combining items.

QC photos are limited visual evidence. Lighting changes apparent color, and photos cannot establish long-term durability or material composition.

## Plan the parcel, not just the item price

Record item costs, seller-to-warehouse delivery, applicable service fees, international shipping, payment/currency charges and applicable taxes or duties. Confirm the currency and billable-weight method. Unknown amounts should remain marked as unknown, rather than silently treated as zero.

Consolidation is a decision to evaluate, not a guarantee of cheaper shipping. Ask how your actual dimensions, packaging and route affect the quote and whether items can travel together.
